Spotify Playlist Taxonomy (Canonical Version)

Owner: Anirudh
Purpose: Canonical classification system for all future song sorting.
Priority Rule: Sonic coherence first, emotional overlap second. If a song sonically belongs somewhere, that outweighs lyrical themes.

⸻

RAP

⸻

MTR (Melodic Trap Rap)

Core Identity

Melody-first trap with atmospheric richness and strong vibe immersion.

Traits

* Heavy autotune/melodies
* Emotional but not necessarily sad
* Spacey production
* Immersive vibe
* Trap drums remain important

Emotional Feel

Night drives, floating, escapism, atmosphere.

Examples

* Wish — Trippie Redd
* Yosemite — Travis Scott
* Company — Don Toliver
* Moonlight — XXXTentacion

Not MTR

* Hard aggressive trap → THR
* Luxury rap → Luxe
* chill reflective rap → MDR

⸻



⸻

THR (Trap Hip-Hop)

Core Identity

Hard trap music focused on energy, momentum, and aggression.

Traits

* Hard drums
* Rapping over melody
* Street energy
* Gym music

Emotional Feel

Aggression, dominance, intensity.

Examples

* Choppa Won’t Miss
* Need It
* Pure Cocaine
* Parking Lot

Not THR

* Rage music → RPM
* Melodic trap → MTR

MDR (Melancholic / Deep Rap)

Classification Rule (Most Important)

When classifying a new song:

1. Sonic identity > Emotional identity
2. Production style > Lyrics
3. Playlist coherence > Artist consistency
4. If a song fits a non-rap playlist better than any rap playlist, do NOT force it into a rap playlist.

This is the canonical version of your Spotify system and is probably the most accurate summary of the distinctions we’ve refined over time
